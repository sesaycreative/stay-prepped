## How the score is calculated

The score covers 10 areas: food, water, emergency supplies, first aid, communication, backup power, household plan, evacuation readiness, financial and essential documents.

### Points for each answer

| Answer | Points |
| --- | --- |
| No | 0 |
| Partly | 1 |
| Mostly | 2 |
| Yes | 3 |

### Category scores

Each category has three questions, so the most it can score is 9 points. We turn that into a score out of 10:

**Category score = points ÷ 9 × 10**, rounded to one decimal place.

For example, answering Yes, Mostly and Partly gives 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 points, which is 6 ÷ 9 × 10 = **6.7 out of 10**.

### Overall score

**Overall score = the average of the 10 category scores × 10**, rounded to the nearest whole number, out of 100. Every category counts equally.

### Bands

| Score | Band |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 39 | Getting started |
| 40 to 69 | Building |
| 70 to 89 | Well prepared |
| 90 to 100 | Highly prepared |

### Strengths and gaps

Your strengths are your three highest-scoring categories (that scored above zero). Your gaps are your three lowest-scoring categories that are not already at 10. Where scores tie, categories appear in the order listed above. Each gap comes with one concrete next step and links to the guide or tool that helps.

## Assumptions and limits

- Every category is weighted equally. In real life, water and warmth usually matter most in the first hours of an emergency, so start there if you are unsure.
- The questions describe a typical UK household. Your situation may need extra planning, for example if someone relies on powered medical equipment.
- The score reflects your own answers. It cannot check what you actually have.
