## How this is calculated

The calculator adds up a daily allowance for each person and pet, then multiplies it by the number of days.

### The planning basis

We plan **4 litres per person per day** in total, for children and adults alike:

- **3 litres for drinking.** This matches the drinking-water figure in the UK government's Be Ready checklist (3 litres per person per day).
- **1 litre for cooking and basic hygiene.**

For comparison, US guidance from Ready.gov uses 1 US gallon per person per day (about 3.8 litres) for drinking and sanitation together. Our 4 litres is close to that, rounded to a figure that is easy to plan with.

### Adjustments

| Situation | Extra per day |
| --- | --- |
| Hot climate or heatwave | +1 litre per person (drinking) |
| Pregnant or breastfeeding | +1 litre per person (drinking) |
| Extra hygiene | +2 litres per person (cooking and hygiene) |
| Small dog | 0.5 litres |
| Medium dog | 1 litre |
| Large dog | 1.5 litres |
| Cat | 0.25 litres |

### Converting units

- Total litres ÷ 4.546 = **UK (imperial) gallons**.
- Total litres ÷ 3.785 = **US gallons**.
- Containers are the total divided by 5, 10 or 25 litres, rounded **up** so you never fall short.

## Assumptions and limits

- These are planning figures. Real needs rise with heat, exercise and illness, so store more if you can.
- Hygiene water here means basic washing. It does not include flushing toilets with mains-style volumes; plan separately if your water supply is likely to be off for long.
- Children are planned at the adult rate. This leaves a little margin for spills and waste.
- Pet figures are rough averages. They are not veterinary advice.
