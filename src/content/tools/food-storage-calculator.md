## How this is calculated

The calculator turns your household into a daily calorie figure, multiplies it by the number of days, and then splits those calories into five food groups. Finally, it converts each group's calories into an approximate weight using typical energy densities for long-life foods.

### Step 1: daily calories

- Each adult (12 and over): **2,000 kcal a day**, the adult reference intake used on UK food labels.
- Each child under 12: **1,500 kcal a day**, a planning average across ages. Younger children need less; older children more.
- High activity multiplies everyone's figure by **1.25**. Choose this if you expect hard physical work, such as clearing up after a storm or flood.

Total calories = (adults × 2,000 + children × 1,500) × activity factor × days.

### Step 2: split by food group

| Food group | Share of calories |
| --- | --- |
| Grains and starches | 40% |
| Protein | 20% |
| Fruit and vegetables | 15% |
| Fats and dairy | 15% |
| Comfort and other | 10% |

This is a simple, balanced split for planning. It is not a dietary prescription.

### Step 3: convert calories to weight

We use these approximate energy densities for common long-life foods:

| Food | kcal per 100 g (or 100 ml) |
| --- | --- |
| Dry rice, pasta or oats | 350 |
| Tinned beans or lentils | 100 |
| Tinned fish or meat | 180 |
| Nut butter | 590 |
| Cooking oil | 900 |
| UHT milk | 60 per 100 ml |
| Tinned or dried fruit and vegetables | 70 |

For fats and dairy, half the calories go to oil and half to UHT milk (or nut butter for a vegan diet). For vegetarian and vegan diets, the protein line is split equally between tinned beans or lentils and nut butter. There is no single density for comfort foods, so we use the dry-goods figure as a stated assumption.

## Assumptions and limits

- Weights are approximate and based on averages; check the label on the products you buy.
- Tinned weights include the liquid in the tin, so real-world tin counts vary.
- The calculator plans calories, not every vitamin and mineral. A varied store with fruit and vegetables helps.
- It does not plan water. Use the [water storage calculator](/tools/water-storage-calculator/) for that.
- If you have no way to cook, choose foods that can be eaten cold or need only hot water.
